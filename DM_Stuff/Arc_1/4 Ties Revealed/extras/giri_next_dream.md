The dream sets you in a dim hallway outside a series of doors to various rooms, the air still and heavy. At the far end stands a young elven woman with tied back auburn hair, tightening a bandolier with various trinkets and tools before locking her door. She looks tired but hopeful, as though preparing for a journey.

The lantern above her flickers. The shadows behind her shift and two masked figures step out of the darkness. The elf turns just as they grab her, one hand covering her mouth, the other pinning her arms. A quick flash of violet runes light the walls, and her body sags helplessly.

They pull a hood over her head and dragged her toward the stairwell.  The dream dissolves, leaving only the echo of their vanishing footsteps.

You hear one of the two masked figures let out a low grumble chuckle and say:

Su laqh vuw bhy qannonb stiw...
