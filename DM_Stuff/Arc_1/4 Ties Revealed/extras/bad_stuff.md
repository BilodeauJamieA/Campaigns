Minor Effects – Escalating Severity (1–20) - animemanniaxx

1. Dusty Cough: A tickle in the throat causes a brief cough at inconvenient moments. No mechanical penalty. 

2. Static Hair: Hair keeps standing up with static. Very slightly noticeable, harmless. 

3. Cold Fingers: Slight chill in the fingertips; requires 1 extra second when picking up small objects. 

4. Echoing Footsteps: Your steps make a faint echo even on soft ground. Stealth checks have a tiny narrative complication (no mechanical penalty unless DM chooses). 

5. Tight Muscles: Mild stiffness; first long stride of the day feels awkward. 

6. Sleepy Eyes: You look a little tired; NPCs may ask if you're okay. 

7. Dry Throat: You need a sip of water every hour or you clear your throat audibly. 

8. Itchy Armor Strap: Something under your gear rubs wrong. Disadvantage on the next Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check only. 

9. Minor Headache: Light tension; −1 on your next Intelligence check. 

10. Loose Buckle: A belt buckle or strap keeps slipping. Movement speed is reduced by 5 ft until you spend an action to tighten it. 

11. Off Balance: Your footing feels slightly uneven. Disadvantage on the first Acrobatics check this session. 

12. Foggy Focus: Harder to concentrate. −1 on concentration checks for the next hour. 

13. Stiff Shoulder: When attacking, your first weapon strike in combat suffers a −1. 

14. Shaky Hands: Fingers tremble slightly. Disadvantage on your next ranged attack made with a weapon. 

15. Tired Feet: Your steps ache. Reduce movement speed by 5 ft for 1 hour. 

16. Muddled Thoughts: Memory feels fuzzy. Disadvantage on any Intelligence-based skill check for 1 hour. 

17. Slight Nausea: Your stomach churns. Disadvantage on Constitution saving throws for 1 hour. 

18. Dizzy Moment: Brief disorientation. You have disadvantage on initiative for the next combat you start. 

19. Short Breath: Something knocks the wind out of you. Lose 2 temporary hit points (or take 2 nonlethal damage). 

20. Minor Magical Drain: A faint magical exhaustion settles over you. You gain one level of Fatigue (custom minor condition: disadvantage on your next ability check, fades after a short rest).
