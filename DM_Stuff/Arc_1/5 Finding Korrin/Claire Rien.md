# Claire Rien
Young High Elf Artificer (Inventor Variant NPC)
Medium humanoid (elf), neutral good

Armor Class 15 (studded leather, reinforced plating)
Hit Points 52 (8d8 + 16)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 8 (-1)
DEX 14 (+2)
CON 14 (+2)
INT 18 (+4)
WIS 12 (+1)
CHA 10 (+0)

Saving Throws INT +7, CON +5
Skills Arcana +7, Investigation +7, Perception +4, Sleight of Hand +5
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
Languages Common, Elvish, Gnomish
Proficiency Bonus +3

## Traits
**Fey Ancestry** Advantage on saving throws against being charmed; immune to magical sleep.

**Inventive Arsenal** Claire carries a limited number of specialized devices. She can deploy 5 charges per encounter, choosing from her gadgets below. Each gadget can only be used if she has charges remaining.

**Rapid Fabrication** Once per short rest, Claire can jury-rig a simple device (lockpick tool, grappling launcher, light source, etc.) as an action.

**Tool Expertise** Double proficiency with tinker's tools and alchemist’s supplies.

## Gadgets
Each use consumes 1 charge unless stated otherwise.

**Flash Charge**
Creatures in a 10-ft radius within 30 ft. must make a DC 15 CON save or be blinded until the end of their next turn.

**Adhesive Bomb**
Creates a sticky 10-ft square within 60 ft. Difficult terrain; creatures entering must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or become restrained (escape DC 13).

**Thermite Capsule**
Ranged attack (+7 to hit, range 60 ft.).
Hit: 3d6 fire damage, and the target takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of its next turn.

**Shock Emitter**
One creature within 30 ft. must make a DC 15 CON save or take 3d8 lightning damage and can’t take reactions until the end of its next turn. Half damage on success.

**Restoration Injector** (2 charges)
A creature Claire touches regains 3d8 + 4 HP.

## Actions

**Arcane Pistol**
Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 80/240 ft., one target.
Hit: 2d10 piercing damage (compressed force bolt).

**Infused Dagger**
Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft.
Hit: 1d4 + 3 piercing + 1d4 force damage.

## Bonus Actions
Quick Deploy (2/day).
Claire uses a gadget as a bonus action instead of an action.

## Reactions
Deflection Field (3/day).
A reactive device grants +4 AC against one attack that would hit her.

## Equipment
Reinforced studded leather, bandolier of gadgets, compact launcher, tinker's tools, alchemist’s kit, half-finished inventions, small clockwork assistant.
