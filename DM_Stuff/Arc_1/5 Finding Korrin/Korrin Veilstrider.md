## Rage Engine (3/Day)

As a bonus action, Korrin enters a mechanical battle-rage for 1 minute.

While raging:

• Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage
• Advantage on Strength checks and saves
• Melee attacks deal +3 damage
• Cannot be charmed or frightened

If Korrin drops below half HP, his Rage Engine automatically activates if it hasn't already.

## Unstoppable Momentum

If Korrin moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hits with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

## Cleaving Arc

Korrin swings his greataxe in a brutal arc.

Each creature within 10 ft must make a DC 17 Dexterity save.

Failure:
22 (2d10) slashing damage

Success:
Half damage.
