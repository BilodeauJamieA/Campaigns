# Magical Relic Ingestion – d100 Permanent Effects

## 1–10 Catastrophic Rejection

* Arcane Scarring – Max HP permanently reduced by 10 as magical burns scar the body.
* Relic Suppression – The original relic becomes dormant and unusable until powerful magic restores it.
* Arcane Instability – Whenever you roll a natural 1, wild magical energy erupts (DM decides effect).
* Soul Strain – Permanently gain –1 to Constitution.
* Magical Rot – Healing received is reduced by 2 each time you are healed.
* Relic Rejection – The body violently expels the new relic and you gain a permanent scar or mutation.
* Psychic Echo – You constantly hear faint whispers from the relics; –1 Wisdom saving throws.
* Cracked Aura – Detect Magic always reveals you as strongly magical and unstable.
* Arcane Parasite – The relic drains vitality; –1 maximum hit die permanently.
* Magical Mutation – Physical change (glowing veins, crystalline skin, etc.) and –1 Charisma.

## 11–19 Negative Resonance

* Magical Weight – Movement speed permanently –5 ft.
* Energy Leakage – You shed faint magical light in darkness.
* Arcane Sensitivity – Disadvantage on one type of saving throw (DM choice).
* Relic Hunger – You occasionally feel compelled to seek magical items.
* Unstable Body – When reduced to 0 HP, roll a DC 10 Con save or suffer 1 level exhaustion when revived.
* Magical Pulse – Nearby animals become uneasy around you.
* Resonant Pain – Casting spells causes 1 damage per spell level.
* Flickering Aura – Illusions and invisibility around you shimmer slightly.
* Relic Echo – Disadvantage on Stealth checks vs magical creatures.

## 20–40 Delayed

* Dormant Relic – The new relic remains inside you but has no effect… yet.

## 41–46 Neutral Transformation

* Magical Mark – Gain a glowing rune or mark tied to the relic.
* Arcane Presence – You always register as magical under detection spells.
* Relic Bond – You can faintly sense the direction of powerful magical items nearby (DM discretion).
* Mystic Resilience – Advantage on one specific type of saving throw once per day.
* Arcane Sight Flicker – You occasionally glimpse magical auras for a moment.
* Relic Awareness – You can feel when your relics are suppressed or dispelled.

## 47–70. Stable 

* Coexistence – Both relics settle in your body with no current effect.

## 71–79 Beneficial Resonance

* Arcane Vitality – Maximum HP +5 permanently.
* Magical Fortitude – +1 Constitution (max 20).
* Arcane Pulse – Once per long rest you can push creatures 5 ft away as a burst of energy.
* Magical Insight – +2 bonus to Arcana checks.
* Energy Absorption – Reduce magical damage taken by 2.
* Relic Conduit – Once per day you can reroll one failed saving throw.
* Spell Resonance – Your first spell each day is cast as one level higher.
* Mystic Agility – +1 Dexterity (max 20).
* Arcane Recovery – Regain 1 additional hit die on long rests.

## 80–90. Stat Bonus

* Harmonized Relics – Both relics hum in balance, granting +1 to a chosen ability score (max 20).

## 91–100 Perfect Relic Harmony

* Twin Relic Core – Gain +1 AC permanently from magical reinforcement.
* Relic Awakening – One relic gains a new minor ability (DM designed).
* Arcane Regeneration – Regain 1 HP at the start of each combat.
* Relic Guardian – Once per long rest, drop to 1 HP instead of 0.
* Magical Surge Core – Once per day gain advantage on all rolls for one round.
* Arcane Body – +10 maximum HP permanently.
* Relic Synergy – Gain +1 to two different ability scores (max 20).
* Living Relic Vessel – Gain resistance to force damage.
* Relic Fusion – The relics partially merge granting a powerful custom ability.
* Ascendant Vessel – You become a true relic host, gaining +2 to one ability score and a unique magical power determined by the DM.
