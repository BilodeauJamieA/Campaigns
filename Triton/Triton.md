# Triton 

## The World

Triton is a land divided not only by class, but by light itself. A massive mountain rises from the center of the island, splitting the world into regions of fleeting daylight and endless shadow. The sun does not cross the sky—it rises in the east, climbs only partway, and falls again where it began. Because of this, half of Triton lives in light, and half has never seen it.

At the mountain’s peak lies Caelestis, a city above the clouds where the Aurelian elite live in perpetual sunlight. Below, the rest of the world struggles beneath war, scarcity, and control. Movement between these worlds is not free. It is controlled, restricted, and, for most, impossible.
Regions of Triton

## Solhaven (Dawnbelt)

Solhaven lies in the Dawnbelt, the only region of Triton that receives direct sunlight. Built across fertile plains and trade routes, it is considered the most stable and livable place below the mountain. The city thrives on commerce, agriculture, and movement, but its prosperity is fragile—resources are taxed heavily, and wealth rarely stays in the hands of those who earn it.

Trade hub of the lowlands
Farmland and open settlements
Strong presence of merchants and laborers

Common peoples include humans, halflings, half-elves, and dragonborn. Fey creatures are known to appear here—some playful, some deceptive, rarely predictable.

## Ironreach Bastion (The Spine)

Ironreach Bastion sits at the base of the great mountain, serving as the primary stronghold of the Enforcers. It is a fortified city of stone, discipline, and control, where movement is monitored and authority is absolute. At its center stands the Ascension Lift—the only known route to Caelestis.

The lift is massive, capable of transporting entire shipments upward into the clouds. It is heavily guarded, rarely used, and inaccessible to nearly all. For most, it serves as a reminder: the path upward exists, but not for them.

Headquarters of the Enforcers
Military training grounds and command centers
Sole access point to the Aurelian Rise

Humans, dwarves, and goliaths are common here. Elementals and constructs are sometimes seen in service to the Aurelians.

## Nocturne Hollow (Gloamlands)

Nocturne Hollow exists in the shadow of the mountain, within the Gloamlands—a region that has never known sunlight. The city is carved into a vast forest basin, illuminated by bioluminescent fungi and faint, artificial light. Life here is harsh and uncertain, shaped by scarcity and constant danger.

Despite this, communities endure. Resistance movements take root in hidden pockets, and those who survive here become resilient, watchful, and difficult to break.

Permanent darkness, lit by natural glow and flame
Resistance activity and hidden settlements
Dangerous wildlife and unpredictable terrain

Drow, tieflings, shadar-kai, and half-orcs are common. Fey creatures exist here as well, but they are more alien and dangerous than their Dawnbelt counterparts.

## Dregmar Port (Black Coast)

Dregmar Port lies along the western edge of Triton, where the shadow deepens into cold waters and endless fog. It is a lawless harbor city, built on trade, smuggling, and secrecy. Power here is not held openly—it is negotiated, bought, and stolen.

Ships come and go, but not all are accounted for. Information is currency, and trust is a liability.

Smuggling, piracy, and black markets
Strong influence from hidden factions
Constant fog and unstable waters

Humans, tieflings, half-orcs, and changelings are common. Strange sea creatures and shadow-touched marine life are frequently encountered.

## Nerathis Deep (Tidemire)

Beneath the waters off the Dawnbelt coast lies Nerathis Deep, a submerged city built into coral structures and ancient ruins. Isolated from the conflicts of the surface, its people live in relative calm—but not without concern. The deep ocean holds secrets, and not all of them are understood.

Nerathis is a place of quiet observation. Its people are aware of the surface world, but rarely intervene.

Underwater city of coral and bioluminescence
Limited resources, strong cultural cohesion
Rumors of ancient beings beneath the depths

Tritons, sea elves, water genasi, and other aquatic peoples call this place home.

## Caelestis (Aurelian Rise)

High above the mountain, beyond the reach of shadow, lies Caelestis. It is a city of marble and gold, bathed in constant sunlight. The Aurelians who live here believe their position is not merely privilege, but proof—they were chosen to rule.

From below, Caelestis appears distant, almost unreal. From within, it is untouched by the suffering that defines the rest of Triton.

Eternal sunlight above the clouds
Seat of Aurelian power and influence
Completely removed from life below

Very few from the lower regions have ever seen it up close. Fewer still have ever set foot within it.
Peoples and Origins


## Races

Different peoples tend to come from different parts of Triton, shaped by the environments they inhabit:

**Solhaven** (Dawnbelt): Humans, halflings, half-elves, dragonborn

**Ironreach** Bastion (Spine): Humans, dwarves, goliaths

**Nocturne Hollow** (Gloamlands): Drow, tieflings, shadar-kai, half-orcs

**Dregmar Port** (Black Coast): Humans, tieflings, changelings, outcasts

**Nerathis Deep** (Tidemire): Tritons, sea elves, water genasi

Fey creatures can be found in both light and shadow, though they differ greatly between regions.
Factions of Triton

## Castes

Though the Aurelians rule from above, life below is shaped by those who endure it.

**Commoners** — The majority of Triton. Survivors, laborers, and everyday people trying to endure a system that does not favor them.

**Enforcers** — Soldiers and veterans who maintain order under Aurelian authority. They believe structure is necessary, even when it comes at a cost.

**Resistance** — Rebels who seek to overthrow both the Enforcers and the Aurelians. They operate in secrecy and fight for change, no matter the price.

**Shadow** — Those who operate outside the system entirely. Smugglers, information brokers, and manipulators who profit from imbalance and control events from behind the scenes.
