## Player Start

Your Place in the World

You are not the most powerful person in Triton. Some would say you are not even important.

But you have been approached with an opportunity.

A promise that your greatest desire will be met.

A choice.

A chance.

The world is finally giving you something it never has before.

Your Character (Session 0)

Please prepare the following:

Level 3 character (point buy system)
Background
Faction (optional, but recommended)
Desire (keep this private from other players)
The last thing you remember doing prior to a private DM interaction....

Note: Most of the campaign will not take place on Triton, but in the event that we should continue beyond my first campaign idea, it would all take place here. 
I hope that's readable. So my idea will not have us interacting with the world of Triton a ton, but I wanted you to have a world to base your character out of and build a desire, or a wish from. I hope that makes sense from the start and like I said, if we wind up finishing the campaign, the next setting I will set up will take place in the world.
